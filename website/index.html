The service

We bring the bar. You bring people worth pouring for.

We are snobs about the drink, never about the guest. Pretension, done properly, is just caring out loud.

01

The bar, built on site

Glassware, tools, spirits, citrus and ice arrive with us and leave with us. You point at a corner. We make it the best one in the room.

02

A menu written for your evening

Every event gets its own list, chosen with you beforehand. Classics made correctly, plus a house recipe or two if you are feeling brave.

03

A live menu on every phone

Guests scan and see what can be made right now. When an ingredient runs out, the drink quietly leaves the menu. Nobody orders a disappointment.
